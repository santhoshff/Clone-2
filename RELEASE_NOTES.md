# Eva Music
## New
- Home screen widget: play, next, like and a sleep timer button.
- Car mode: huge buttons and voice commands. Can open by itself when your phone joins the car Bluetooth.
- Follow artists: follow from the artist page, see albums, get an alert for new songs.
- Themes: colours from the cover art or your own colour, with black, grey or light backgrounds.
- Find a song: sing, hum or say a few words and Eva suggests songs.
- Karaoke: words light up in time, and you can turn the lead vocal down.
- Eva Wrapped: your year with top song, minutes and favourite language.
- Import playlists: paste a YouTube or Spotify link, or a list of songs.
